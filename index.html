01 / THE PROCESS

How it works

A simple closed-loop control system: sense, decide, act, verify.

1

Sense

The soil moisture sensor continuously reads moisture and sends it to the Arduino Nano.

2

Decide

If moisture drops below 30%, the Arduino triggers the relay. The RTC also double-checks every 30 minutes.

3

Water

The relay switches the pump on and water is supplied to the soil of the plant box, only while it is needed.

4

Stop

At 80% moisture the Arduino turns the relay off. No overwatering, no waste.

05 / THE COMPONENTS

Hardware

Simple, low-cost parts anyone can source. Tap a component to learn more.

Arduino Nano

What it is: A small, low-power microcontroller board that runs our program.

How it works: It reads sensor inputs, compares them with the limits we set, and switches outputs on or off.

In our project: The brain. It turns the pump on below 30% moisture and off at 80%.

Soil moisture sensor

What it is: A probe that is pushed into the soil.

How it works: Its electrical signal changes with the water content of the soil. The Nano reads it and converts it to a 0 to 100% value.

In our project: It tells the system how dry or wet the soil is, all the time.

RTC module

What it is: A Real Time Clock, a tiny chip that keeps accurate time, with a small backup battery.

How it works: The Nano asks it for the time over a simple two-wire link.

In our project: It shows the time on the display and triggers the scheduled moisture check every 30 minutes.

0.96" OLED display

What it is: A small, bright screen that draws its own light.

How it works: The Nano sends it text and simple pictures to show.

In our project: It shows the time, soil moisture %, pump status, and a face icon (sad, neutral or happy) so anyone can read the plant's condition at a glance.

Relay module

What it is: An electrically controlled switch.

How it works: A small signal from the Nano closes the switch for the pump circuit. The Nano pin cannot power a pump directly, and the relay keeps the two circuits separate.

In our project: It turns the water pump on and off automatically.

Water pump

What it is: A small electric pump that moves water through a pipe.

How it works: When the relay closes, the pump runs and pushes water from the tank to the plants.

In our project: It waters the soil only when moisture is low and stops once it reaches 80%.

Rechargeable battery

What it is: The power source for the whole system.

How it works: It stores energy and can be recharged when it runs low.

In our project: It makes the setup portable and independent of a wall socket. Solar charging is a planned upgrade.
